Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026.

Dollar Search is a website where friends share research portfolios. This page explains what we keep about you and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, so you can sign in. If you sign in with Google, we get your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We only use your email to sign you in.
  • What you post: your portfolio, character, research, comments, reactions, likes, follows, polls and votes. These are public unless the site says otherwise (like private drafts).
  • Private things: messages with friends, saved research, calendar plans, team notes, blocks and reports. Only you (and, for messages and team notes, the people they're shared with) can see these. Reports are seen by the site owner.
  • On your device: small settings like dark mode and whether you've seen the welcome tour, stored in your browser.

How it's used

Only to run the site: showing portfolios, letting you sign in, and keeping private things private. We don't sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it's kept

The site's data is stored with Supabase, and the website is hosted on Vercel. Research you link to (like Google Docs or YouTube videos) is shown from those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your things

You can delete your research, comments and portfolio at any time from the site. To delete your whole account, contact the site owner and it will be removed.
