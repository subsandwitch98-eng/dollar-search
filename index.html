Get started

How to get your research on Dollar Search and make it easy for people to read.

  1. 1

    Make your portfolio

    Sign in, click + Create your profile, then build your character with Edit character. Everyone gets one portfolio.

  2. 2

    Add a research card

    On your portfolio, click + Add a research project. Give it a clear title (up to 80 characters) and a short description (up to 400).

  3. 3

    Link your work

    Paste the full web address, like https://…. For Google Docs, set sharing to Anyone with the link so visitors can open it.

  4. 4

    Show your sources

    In the card's editor, click + Add a source for each one. Give each source a name and a link. Visitors see them under the Sources tab.

  5. 5

    Keep it easy to scan

    Start the description with your main finding in one sentence. Then say how you found it. Short beats long.

  6. 6

    Get feedback and team up

    Others can like your research and leave comments or tips. To work with someone, open their portfolio and click Request collab.
